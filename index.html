Straight answers

Are you a law firm or customs broker?

No. We review billing documents and cite the published regulation. We don't give legal advice, represent you before the FMC, or file anything with US Customs. For legal questions, talk to your counsel.

Who sends the disputes?

You do, under your own name. We prepare drafts and track deadlines. Whether to dispute, pay, or hold any invoice is always your call.

Where are you based?

We operate from India, which is why invoices you send in the evening are usually reviewed by your next morning. Every engagement can start with a mutual NDA.

What happens to our invoices?

They're stored encrypted, used only for your review, never shared with another customer, and deleted within 90 days after we stop working together, or sooner if you ask.

Does this work if our forwarder bills us?

Yes. The billing rule also covers NVOCC invoices. For those, the 30-day clock runs from the date of the carrier invoice your forwarder received.

What if our invoices are all compliant?

Then the free review confirms it and you've lost nothing. Carriers improve their invoices over time, and we'll tell you honestly if ongoing reviews aren't worth it for you.
